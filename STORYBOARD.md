# "30 Days Lit": beat-by-beat storyboard

84 BPM, so 1 beat is 0.714 s and 1 bar is 2.86 s. The film is 40½ bars long (115.7 s). Beats are real beats, numbered from 0.
**Bold** marks a scene change: something transforms and a hit plays in the soundtrack.

There are no glows anywhere. Shadows are short contact shadows that are always darker than the background. Large wipes are drawn at the frame's own time, so their edges stay crisp. Big moves use springs, so motion blur never smears bright shapes into a halo.

The 3D renders from the Salam file appear at hero moments only: the threat cards, the check on "a reason to stay", and the glass logo in the hook, the roof and the app icon. Every other icon is a simple Hugeicons line icon.

## ACT I: Kinetic type (beats 0–27)
| Beats | What happens |
|---|---|
| **0–3** (hook) | Frame 0 is the Salam app icon alone on Night. A feed of verb cards (CHAT · WATCH · PAY · PLAY · SHOP · LISTEN · SCROLL · STREAM) whips past and brakes on the Salam card: "Last opened **29 days ago**". Then "Once a **month.**" slams in. |
| **4–7** | "They live on their phones" next to a real Salam customer, then "EVERY" and "**DAY.**" The full stop grows into the next scene. |
| **8–15** | "Disruption from / **every direction.**" Five threat cards, each sized to its text, fly in and squeeze the glass logo. |
| **16–19** | "The risk is real." → "The opportunity is" → "**bigger.**" The Salam mark opens like an iris into Deep Green. |
| **20–27** | "Awareness / isn't the problem." is struck through, then "**Relevance** is." Then "Winning apps are built on / **daily habits** / ~~not monthly utility.~~" |

| **36–39** | Thirty days of "Winning apps" (every day lit) against the "Telco app" (only bill day lit). |

"IDENTITY LAYER / Nafath · Riyadh", then "Telecoms own the identity layer." A verified tick lands, then "Trusted identity and connectivity **at scale.**" The rings gather into the tile. |

## ACT III: The Strategy House and EAI, all flat (beats 59–91)

|---|---|

| Beats | What happens |

| **100–111** | Usage bars beside the real Salam home screen. Then 72% and four barriers that flip to their answers. |
| **112–115** | "Turn every reason not to choose us / **into a reason to stay.**" The card breaks into fragmented tiles that pour into one phone. |
| **116–123** | The hub: "One App. **One Experience.**" Six services plug into the phone, AI ties it together, and two more phones slide out. Every line leaves before the next scene. |

## ACT V: Step by step, vision, end title (beats 123–161)
| Beats | What happens |
|---|---|
| **123–124** (as the reference) | The phones drop away. On Mist with dashed orbit guides and a faint dot grid: "THE PLAN · STEP BY STEP", the wordmark top right, "BECOME A SUPERAPP / Step by Step **Approach**", a 01–02–03 stepper and a progress line along the bottom. |
| **125–127** | **The Core** sphere springs in (phone and home icons), with "PHASE 01 · CORE / Merge Mobile + Home" and a "Unified account" callout. |
| **127½–129** | **Beyond** grows out from behind the Core as a dark ring with six partner icons, with "PHASE 02 · BEYOND / Open up to partners" and "AI-guided discovery". |
| **130–132** | **Ecosystem** grows out as a neon ring with eight white icon tiles, with "PHASE 03 · ECOSYSTEM / Build Salam-owned platforms" and "Social & first-party content". |
| **132½–135** | An orbit closes around all three rings and the title becomes "Core → Beyond → **Ecosystem**". A deep green disc opens from the rings. |
| **136–143** | "Beyond Telco. / **Closer to Life.**" beside real customers. A Mist sunrise rises from the arc (crisp edge) into the light-themed "Everyday moments. / **All in one place.**" |
| **144–151** | The moments become 30 days on deep green. The counter climbs under the riser, and "once a month" is struck out by "**every day.**" |
| **152** (hit) | All 30 days are lit, and the month punches outward. |
| **152½–155** (end card, as the reference) | Concentric guides draw. The 30 days become icon tiles orbiting the centre, then spiral into the real "salam" wordmark. |
| **156–159** (last full bar, the strongest moment) | "Making **the joy of technology**". A neon highlight sweeps behind the phrase and turns the type deep green. Then "accessible for everyone, everyday." (with "everyday." underlined) and "BEYOND TELCO · CLOSER TO LIFE", while small moments orbit and the progress line fills. |
| **160–161** (final half bar, end title) | The lines clear, the wordmark shrinks into the label, and a night disc closes over the frame. The app icon returns, so the last frame is the first frame and the film loops. |
